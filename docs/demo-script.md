# Demo video script (about 80 seconds)

Record the live site at 1440 px wide in the light theme, with the season set to 2025-26 and nothing selected yet. Read every number off the screen while recording; the ones below are what the site showed in October 2026.

| Time | Say | Show on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:08 | **Hook.** "In 2015, California lost about half its pistachio crop. The trees didn't get enough winter cold." | Title card: "Counting the Cold". Then the dashboard, map of California. |
| 0:08 to 0:20 | **Problem.** "Growers track that cold, called chill, from weather stations. But stations are far apart, and they close. This one in Durham shut down in July 2025, and the orchards around it lost their numbers." | Zoom the map to Butte County. Hover the dashed **Durham #12, closed** marker. |
| 0:20 to 0:32 | **Feature 1: any orchard.** "So we estimate chill and heat for any orchard in California. Here's one next to the closed station: 74 chill portions last winter, 19 percent below normal, and every number shows how far off it typically is." | Click **Butte orchard near Durham**. Scroll past the bloom card to the four metric cards. Circle "▼19.0% vs 10-season median" and "± 4 CP expected". |
| 0:32 to 0:52 | **Feature 2: what to do.** "But a grower really wants one answer: will my trees bloom on time? Tom grows pistachios. Here, yes. Near Bakersfield, last winter was borderline, so it tells him exactly what to do: check weekly, and by mid-January ask his farm advisor about dormant oil or kaolin clay." | In **Will my trees bloom on time?**, pick **Pistachio**: green **Enough**. Then tap the map near Bakersfield: the card turns amber (**Borderline**, about 60 against a need of 54 to 65). Scroll through **What to do**. |
| 0:52 to 1:02 | **Feature 3: proof it's accurate.** "We tested ourselves against nine real weather stations. Our station correction cuts the heat error in half, and a closed station's records still calibrate the orchards around it." | Click **How accurate is this?** and press **Play**: the amber bubbles shrink and turn blue. Point at the Durham bubble. |
| 1:02 to 1:12 | **Offline and text-style chat.** "It works without signal in the orchard, and growers can just ask: type 'durham' and get the summary in plain words." | Phone view (390 px): airplane-mode icon on, the saved orchard opens with the offline banner. Then the **Ask about an orchard** box: click **Durham**. |
| 1:12 to 1:20 | **Close.** "Counting the Cold. Your orchard's chill and heat, not a station's." | End card: the live URL, counting-the-cold-jfm3.vercel.app, and "2nd Overall, 1st in Track, Reboot Earth California @ UC Davis 2026". |

## Recording tips

- Open `/api/health` on the backend a minute before recording so the first click isn't slow.
- Close the first-visit guide (or record it as the opener) and set **Your crop** to Almond before you start, so Feature 2 can switch to Pistachio.
- Record each row as its own clip and cut them together; it is easier than one perfect take.
- Keep the cursor still while you talk, and move it only to point at what you're saying.
- If the numbers on screen differ from the ones above (the current season keeps changing), say the on-screen ones.
